# Section One logo concepts

Three early identity directions generated for comparison. These are raster concept boards, not final production artwork.

1. **Signal One** — a numeral `1` cut into the `O`; the clearest standalone icon.
2. **Section Frame** — an `SO` monogram organized by a central field/hash line; the strongest sports-system direction.
3. **Cutline One** — editorial rules interrupted by a decisive orange `1`; the strongest publication/technology direction.

Signal One was selected as the primary identity. Production assets are exact pixel extractions from the approved generated canvases; they are not redrawn approximations. They live in `public/brand/`:

- `signal-one-canvas.png` — untouched approved Signal One canvas
- `logo-primary-wordmark.png` — exact horizontal lockup crop
- `logo-primary-mark.png` — exact standalone Signal One crop
- `favicon.png` — exact small-scale mark from the Signal One canvas
- `logo-alternate-section-frame.png` — exact shortened Section Frame crop
- `section-frame-canvas.png` — untouched approved Section Frame canvas

Color alternates mirror the same four filenames under `public/brand/alternates/`:

- `orange/` — house orange on warm ivory
- `reversed/` — warm ivory on midnight navy; Section Frame retains its orange divider
- `monochrome/` — black on white
- `transparent/` — midnight navy on alpha; Section Frame retains its orange divider

Favicon contrast candidates live in `public/brand/favicon-candidates/`:

- `faithful-transparent.png` — original navy tile with the enclosed ivory counter preserved
- `inverted-transparent.png` — ivory tile with a navy counter
- `comparison-light-dark.png` — both candidates shown on light and dark browser-chrome colors
